Time Sense Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Time Sense is an alarm and daily routine app for Android, operated by Schema Sense Studio LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information Time Sense collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Time Sense works fully with no account and no network connection. An account is optional and exists only to back up and sync your own alarm list.

Information we collect

On your phone. Everything you create in Time Sense is stored on your device: your alarm items (label, reason, next action, time and days, sound and strength, and whether the item is a task), the history of each item (when it fired and how you answered: done, later, rescheduled, missed, cancelled, rest, and so on), and your app settings. This information stays on your phone unless you create an account. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Account information. If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address and a password through our sync provider, Supabase, so you can sign in. The account is optional. Without one, no information leaves your phone at all.

Your alarm activity, when you sync. If you are signed in, your alarm items and your end state records (how each alarm firing ended) sync to your account on Supabase, so your list is backed up and stays the same when you sign in again. Sync traffic is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.

Connect your AI keys. Signed in users may create an API key inside the app and give that key to their own AI assistant. With your key, your AI can read your alarm items and propose changes. A proposed change never applies itself: every change waits in the app until you approve or reject it. You can revoke a key at any time in the app, which cuts that AI off immediately. We never give your key or your data to any AI service; you hand your key to your own assistant yourself.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no third party analytics or tracking of any kind. The app contains no ads.
  • No location, contacts, messages, photos, files, calendar, or health data. The app does not ask for access to any of them.
  • No payment information. The app takes no payments and holds no card or bank details.

How we use information

We use the information above only to operate Time Sense: ringing your alarms on your phone, signing you in, backing up and syncing your own list, and keeping the service secure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else processes it

Supabase runs the optional account and sync service. It stores the synced data described above and processes it only to provide that service. No one else. There are no advertisers, analytics companies, or data brokers in the app.

Data retention and deletion

Your synced data is kept while your account exists. On your phone, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings and everything stored on the device is gone. To delete your account and all of its synced data, follow the steps on our account deletion page. To delete your synced data while keeping your account, see our data deletion page.

Children

Time Sense is a general purpose productivity tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Anyone who uses the app without an account sends us no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and post the new version at this same address before the change applies to the app.
