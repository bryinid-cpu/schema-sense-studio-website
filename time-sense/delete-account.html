Delete Your Time Sense Account

Time Sense, by Schema Sense Studio LLC

You can ask us to delete your Time Sense account and everything synced to it. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to help@schemasensestudio.com from the email address on your Time Sense account.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Time Sense account.
  3. We verify the request came from the account's email address, then delete the account and all of its synced data within 30 days. We send a confirmation email when the deletion is complete.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your email address and sign in credentials.
  • All of your synced alarm items and their histories.
  • Your end state records (how each alarm firing ended).
  • Any Connect your AI keys on the account. Deleting the account also cuts off any AI you connected.

What is not deleted by this request

Data stored only on your phone is not on our servers and is not touched by this request. To remove it, uninstall Time Sense or clear its data in Android settings.

If you never created an account

Time Sense works fully without an account. If you never created one, we hold no account and no synced data for you, and there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything on your phone.
