Delete Your Time Sense Data, Keep Your Account

Time Sense, by Schema Sense Studio LLC

You can delete the data synced to your Time Sense account without deleting the account itself.

Option 1: Delete items in the app

Delete any alarm item inside Time Sense and the deletion syncs to your account: the item and its history are removed from our servers as well as from your phone. This is the fastest way to remove specific items.

Option 2: Ask us to wipe your synced data

  1. Send an email to help@schemasensestudio.com from the email address on your Time Sense account.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Time Sense data.
  3. We verify the request came from the account's email address, then delete all synced alarm items, histories, and end state records on the account within 30 days. We send a confirmation email when it is done.

What gets deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: all synced alarm items and their histories, and all end state records.
  • Kept: your account itself (your email address and sign in), so you can sign in and start fresh. Any Connect your AI keys stay active unless you revoke them in the app.
  • Not affected: data stored only on your phone. Items still on your phone will sync again the next time the app syncs, so delete them in the app first if you want them gone everywhere.

To delete the account itself as well, see our account deletion page.
